♟ Chess Rules & Piece Guide
♙ Pawn: 1 square forward; from its starting rank it may move 2. Captures 1 square diagonally forward.
♘ Knight: Moves in an L-shape: 2 squares one way + 1 perpendicular. It can jump over pieces.
♗ Bishop: Moves any number of squares diagonally, but cannot jump over pieces.
♖ Rook: Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically, but cannot jump.
♕ Queen: Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically or diagonally, without jumping.
♔ King: Moves 1 square in any direction. It may never move into or remain in check.
Pawn = 1Knight = 3Bishop = 3Rook = 5Queen = 9King = no point value

Special rules: castling, en passant and pawn promotion are supported. Checkmate wins; stalemate, threefold repetition, insufficient material and the 50-move rule can produce a draw. White moves first.
